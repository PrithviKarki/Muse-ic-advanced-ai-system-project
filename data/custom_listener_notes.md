# Custom Listener Curation Notes (`custom_listener_notes.md`)

- **Pomodoro & Exam Week Note:** For exam week, debugging sessions, and late-night Python coding, prioritize smooth sophisti-pop and rainy City Pop tracks by **Sade Adu** (*Paradise*, *The Sweetest Taboo*, *Smooth Operator*) and **Japanese City Pop** (*Shibuya After Rain*) because their rain textures and velvet Rhodes piano keep focus steady.
- **Tokyo Night Drive & Retro City Pop Note:** For late-night highway drives or upbeat groove sessions, pair **Mariya Takeuchi** (*Plastic Love*) with **Miki Matsubara** (*真夜中のドア〜stay with me*) and **Anri** (*Remember Summer Days*) for authentic 1980s Japanese City Pop momentum.
- **Peak Gym & High-BPM Groove Note:** When running cardio sprints or hyping up an upbeat session, prioritize **Yasuha** (*Fly-day Chinatown*), **Stevie Wonder** (*Part-Time Lover*), and **Billy Ocean** (*Caribbean Queen*).
- **Late-Night 4:00 A.M. Introspection Note:** When in a somber or melancholic late-night mood, **Taeko Ohnuki** (*4:00 A.M.*) and **Tomoko Aran** (*Midnight Pretenders*) offer deeply atmospheric nocturnal arrangements.
