# Custom Listener Curation Notes (`custom_listener_notes.md`)

- **Pomodoro & Exam Week Note:** For exam week, debugging sessions, and late-night Python coding, prioritize tracks by **Paper Lanterns** (*Library Rain*, *Rainy Windowpane*) and **LoRoom** (*Midnight Coding*, *Focus Flow*) because their rain textures and Rhodes piano keep focus steady without vocal distraction.
- **Morning Espresso & Reading Note:** When brewing coffee or reading on a quiet morning, **Slow Stereo** (*Coffee Shop Stories*, *Afterhours Espresso*) and **The Cedar Woods** (*Mountain Pine Dawn*) provide warm acoustic upright bass and fingerstyle guitar.
- **Highway & Gaming Sprint Note:** For neon night drives or gaming marathons, pair **Neon Echo** (*Night Drive Loop*) with **Overdrive X** (*Cybernetic Pulse*) for continuous 80s analog synth momentum.
- **Existential & Cathartic Listening Note:** When feeling overwhelmed or in a somber mood, **Blind River Sam** (*Midnight Delta Blues*) and **Clara Vane** (*Moonlight Sonata Flow*) offer deeply expressive acoustic instrumentation.
